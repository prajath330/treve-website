About TREVE

 

TREVE is a refreshing beverage made with natural Ceylon cinnamon and purified water, inspired by Sri Lanka's rich cinnamon heritage.

 

Why Choose TREVE?

 
  • Made with natural Ceylon cinnamon
  • No artificial flavours or colours
  • No added sugar
  • No preservatives
  • Made in Sri Lanka
  • Refreshing and convenient
 

Our Story

 

Having grown cinnamon for more than ten years, we wanted to create a value-added product that showcases the goodness of true Ceylon cinnamon to Sri Lanka and the world.
